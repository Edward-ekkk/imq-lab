Yinuo Zhou receives a graduate entrance scholarship

Award date · 1 September 2026

Yinuo Zhou has received a Chow Yei Ching School of Graduate Studies Entrance Scholarship from City University of Hong Kong. The university's award record gives the award date as 1 September 2026.

Yinuo is a doctoral researcher in Systems Engineering and a member of IMQ Lab. Congratulations, Yinuo, on this recognition at the beginning of your doctoral journey.
