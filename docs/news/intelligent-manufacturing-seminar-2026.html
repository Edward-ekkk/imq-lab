Prof. Meng co-organises a seminar on intelligent manufacturing

Event date · 31 January 2026

Prof. Rainers Yuquan Meng served as a co-organiser of the joint seminar on intelligent manufacturing held by the CityU EngD Society and the Hong Kong Branch of the Institution of Mechanical Engineers (IMechE).

The university's activity record dates the event to 31 January 2026 in Hong Kong and lists W. M. Kong as Advisory Chair. The seminar's focus connects with the lab's interest in intelligent manufacturing systems.
