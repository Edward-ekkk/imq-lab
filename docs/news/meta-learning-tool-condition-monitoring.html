Meta-learning research published in IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics

Journal issue · January 2025

A paper by Yuquan Meng, Zhiqiao Dong, Kuan-Chieh Lu, Shichen Li and Chenhui Shao appears in the January 2025 issue of IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics, volume 21, issue 1, pages 653–662. It was first published online on 23 September 2024.

The study develops a similarity-based meta-representation learning approach for tool condition monitoring. It examines how knowledge learned from existing welding scenarios can transfer to other scenarios, reducing the need to collect labelled data for each new configuration.
