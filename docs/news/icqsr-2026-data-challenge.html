IMQ members receive an ICQSR 2026 Data Challenge Finalist Award

Award date · 5 July 2026

Congratulations to Longjun Zhang, Xiang Yang and Prof. Rainers Yuquan Meng on their Finalist Award in the Data Challenge at the 2026 INFORMS Conference on Quality, Statistics, and Reliability (ICQSR).

CityUHK lists all three as recipients of the award, dated 5 July 2026, and records the recognition as international. This is a shared achievement for our doctoral researchers and their supervisor.
