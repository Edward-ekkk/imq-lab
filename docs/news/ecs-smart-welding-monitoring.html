ECS project begins on intelligent monitoring of ultrasonic welding

Project start · 1 January 2026

An ECS project led by Prof. Rainers Yuquan Meng began on 1 January 2026. The project, When Machines Learn to See – A Smart Online Monitoring Approach for Ultrasonic Metal Welding, is registered at CityUHK under project number 9048328.

The research aims to use Kolmogorov–Arnold Networks to work with complex sensor signals, make monitoring models easier to interpret, and adapt to previously unseen process conditions. These are research objectives, with potential applications in joining dissimilar materials for electric vehicles and lightweight structures.
