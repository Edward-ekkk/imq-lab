01 / RESEARCH

Where learning meets making.

We study how data, physical knowledge and intelligent monitoring can work together in manufacturing.

01LEARNINGData-efficient intelligence

Developing learning methods that adapt across manufacturing conditions with limited labeled data. Our published work explores meta-learning, few-shot learning and domain generalization.

Meta-learningFew-shot learningDomain generalization
Related publication↗
02PHYSICAL KNOWLEDGEPhysics-informed machine learning

Bringing physical understanding into machine learning for manufacturing. The research connects process knowledge with data-driven models, including online prediction of joint strength in ultrasonic metal welding.

Physics-informed learningQuality prediction
Research profile↗
03MANUFACTURING SYSTEMSIntelligent monitoring & control

Studying in-situ sensing, anomaly detection and smart decision-making for manufacturing systems. Related interests include automation and 3D metrology.
