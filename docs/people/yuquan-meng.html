Principal Investigator · Assistant Professor

Rainers Yuquan Meng

孟雨泉

IMQ Lab · Department of Systems Engineering · CityUHK

Prof. Meng joined City University of Hong Kong in 2024. His research connects data-efficient learning, physics-informed machine learning and manufacturing process monitoring and control. He received his Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign in 2023 and his bachelor's degree from the University of Science and Technology of China in 2017.
